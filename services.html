Services

Four services, priced as one.

You can take all four, or just the one you are missing. Most first orders start with sourcing and inspection, then grow into consolidation once there are three or more suppliers.

01 — Sourcing

Find the supplier, not just the listing

A listing photo and a factory are two different things. We match your spec against suppliers who actually stock it, ask for quotes in writing, and tell you what each price really includes.

Turnaround: quotes back within 24 hours for lists up to ten items
  • Search on 1688, Taobao, Tmall and Pinduoduo by photo, link or keyword
  • Price comparison across at least three suppliers where they exist
  • Sample ordering before a bulk run, with sample cost quoted up front
  • MOQ, lead time and customisation (logo, packaging, labels) confirmed in writing
  • Supplier background check: years trading, factory or trading company, main markets

You get: a comparison table, supplier contacts, and a recommendation with reasons.

02 — Inspection

Check before the balance goes out

Most disputes are not fraud, they are small differences nobody checked: 480 pieces instead of 500, a shade off, a seam that splits. We look before you pay the rest.

Done at our Dongguan warehouse, or at the factory for large runs
  • Count, weight and carton dimensions against the order
  • Photo and video record of the actual goods, not catalogue images
  • Defect notes with an accept / rework / return recommendation
  • Barcode, label and packaging check for your market
  • Written report you can forward to your own customer

You get: a photo report per SKU, plus a one-page summary of what to accept.

03 — Consolidation

Several suppliers, one shipment, one invoice

Buying from five suppliers means five freight quotes, five boxes and five sets of documents. We receive everything at one address, merge it, and ship it once.

Free storage 30 days · Longer storage quoted per pallet
  • Receiving and checking each inbound delivery against your list
  • Merging cartons, removing excess packaging to cut chargeable weight
  • Re-labelling with your SKU codes or retail-ready labels
  • Palletising and wrapping for sea freight, or piece-count packing for air
  • One commercial invoice and packing list covering all suppliers

You get: final weights, volume, carton list and photos of the packed shipment.

04 — Export & freight

Booked on a route that fits the goods

Air for samples and light high-value goods, sea for volume, courier for small parcels. We quote the options with transit times instead of picking one for you.

Customs clearance at destination is handled by your broker or by our partner
  • Air, sea LCL/FCL and express, quoted side by side
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and shipping marks prepared
  • Booking, loading supervision and container photos
  • Tracking numbers and milestone updates until delivery
  • Consolidation with other shipments to reach a better rate

You get: a freight quote with transit time, plus documents and tracking.

What we do not do

Clear boundaries save both sides a wasted month.

  • We do not manufacture. We buy from factories and marketplaces on your behalf.
  • We do not provide credit, letters of credit or financing. Goods are paid for before purchase.
  • We do not guarantee customs outcomes. We prepare correct documents and tell you what your country will ask for.
  • We do not handle regulated goods — medicines, batteries above courier limits, food, cosmetics with registration requirements — unless we confirm in writing first.
  • We do not mark up freight invisibly. If a rate changes, you see the new rate and the reason.

Tell us what you are trying to buy.

One link or one photo is enough to start. We will tell you whether it is worth importing at your volume.
