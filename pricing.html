Pricing

One fee on the goods. Everything else at cost.

We charge a percentage of the goods value. Freight, duties and destination charges are quoted separately, with the carrier's rate shown next to ours.

Service fee

Based on goods value before freight. Invoiced in USD.

Order value (goods) Service fee Notes
Under $1,000 9% Minimum $60 per order
$1,000 – $5,000 7% Most first orders land here
Above $5,000 5% Repeat and seasonal orders
Sourcing only, no purchase $40 Supplier shortlist and quotes, no goods bought

Trial orders under $300: flat $60 fee so that a test costs you a test, not a project.

What the fee covers

Included, and deliberately not included.

Included

  • Product search and written quotes from suppliers
  • Purchasing on your behalf, including platform accounts
  • Receiving, counting and photo reporting at our warehouse
  • Consolidation, re-packing, labelling and palletising
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and shipping marks
  • Freight booking and tracking updates
  • Storage up to 30 days after goods arrive

Billed separately

  • Freight and insurance, at the carrier's rate
  • Import duties, VAT and destination customs fees
  • Bank and payment processing fees
  • Samples, and any sample freight
  • Rework or re-packing at a supplier's request
  • Storage beyond 30 days, $6 per pallet per day
  • Factory visits outside Dongguan, travel at cost

How payment works

Goods are paid before purchase. Nothing else is charged until it happens.

01

Quote approved

You confirm the quote in writing, and we send an invoice for goods plus service fee. We do not buy before it is settled.

02

Goods purchased

Orders are placed with each supplier and lead times confirmed. Delays are reported the moment we hear them.

03

Inspection report sent

Photos, counts and defects go to you. Rework or returns are raised with suppliers before shipping.

04

Freight invoiced

Freight is invoiced at booking with the carrier's rate attached. Balance is due before departure.

Accepted: bank transfer (T/T) and Wise. Repeat buyers can discuss 70/30 terms.
