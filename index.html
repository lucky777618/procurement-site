Four things we take off your desk

Most buyers lose time on the middle part — between finding a supplier and getting a box at the door. That is the part we run.

Sourcing

Send a photo, a link or a spec. We find matching suppliers, ask for real quotes, and tell you where the price differences come from.

Inspection

We count, measure, photograph and record defects before you approve the balance. You see what we see.

Consolidation

Six suppliers, one shipment. We receive, check, re-pack, label and palletise, then send you a single weight and volume.

Export & freight

Air, sea or courier, booked in your name or ours. Commercial invoice, packing list and tracking come with every shipment.

From your list to your door

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them.

01

Send your list

Product links, photos or a written spec, plus quantity and target price. A spreadsheet is fine; a chat message is fine too.

Usually 5–10 minutes of your time
02

Get a landed-cost quote

Unit price, service fee and a freight estimate, in one table. If a supplier looks wrong, we say so before you commit.

Back within 24 hours

Everything is merged, re-packed and booked onto the agreed route. You get weights, packing list and tracking.
