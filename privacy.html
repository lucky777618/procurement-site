Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Short version: your enquiry details, kept to answer your enquiry and run your order. Nothing else.

What we collect

When you send a quote request, we receive the name, company, email, destination country and order details you type into the form. Our server logs may also record your IP address and browser type for security and abuse prevention.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To run an order once you place one.
  • To keep records required for invoicing and customs documentation.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your details.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list without a clear opt-in.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this site. There is no analytics script on these pages.

Who else sees it

Suppliers receive only what is needed to quote or produce your goods — usually a product description and quantity, not your contact details. Freight carriers and customs brokers receive the information required on shipping documents.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become orders are deleted within 12 months. Order records are kept as long as tax and customs rules require.

Your choices

Write to hello@lucky7link.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We answer within 30 days.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this domain, not from a font CDN.
