Before you send

These three details make a quote accurate rather than optimistic.

01

Destination and postcode

Freight changes with the port or airport. A city name is enough to start; a postcode makes the estimate real.

02

Quantity and pack size

Whether it ships as retail units or bulk cartons decides packaging work and chargeable weight.

03

Your deadline

A date changes the route: sea is cheap and slow, air is fast and priced per kilo. Tell us the date you actually need it.
